# Hi, I'm Ingrid 👋

AI engineer and web developer with a data science background, based in Germany.
I build tools that keep your data on your own device, for law, public services and media.
I also care about civic tech, migration and public space.

## What I'm working on

| Project | What it does | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Cassette**](https://github.com/ingridstevens/cassette) | Turns a recording into a transcript that says who spoke. Runs offline, with no account and no telemetry. German, English, French and Spanish. | TypeScript, Python, Whisper |
| [**Not A Lawyer**](https://github.com/ingridstevens/lawyer) | Explains German residency and citizenship law in your language. Quotes the official law text and uses a local model, so your questions never leave your Mac. | Swift, SwiftUI, RAG, local LLM |
| [**Florestan**](https://github.com/ingridstevens/florestan) | A Mac menu bar app that turns a YouTube link into a song or playlist in Apple Music. Install with Homebrew. Public domain. | Swift |

The thread running through all three: **useful AI that doesn't need your data to leave your machine.**
